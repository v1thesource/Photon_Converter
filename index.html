About Alpes Lasers Photon Wavelength Converter

This high-precision tool quickly converts between standard units of photon wavelength, energy, frequency, and wavenumber. It is designed for quantum cascade laser (QCL) researchers, laser engineers, and spectroscopists working across mid-infrared and terahertz bands.

Conversion Formulas & Physics Constants:
  • Wavelength to Wavenumber: cm⁻¹ = 10,000 / µm
  • Energy to Wavenumber (Alpes): cm⁻¹ = eV / 0.000123984193
  • Frequency to Wavenumber (Speed of Light): cm⁻¹ = THz / 0.0299792458
Advanced Features:
  • Bidirectional real-time input: Modify any field and see all other units update instantly.
  • Gas Sensing Presets: Quickly load typical absorption lines of molecules like CO₂, CH₄, etc.
  • Interactive Spectrum Visualizer: See the active region and where it lies on the electromagnetic band.
  • Conversion History: Track your calculations and copy values with one click.
